    2026-10-25 · Radial map: from the star to the rings¶

    Duration: afternoon Commits: cf408e4 (radial map) · 231e2cc (rings, more bosses and zoom/pan) · 98b6304 (simulations and docs) · 4bd2079 (room distribution) · 7f00972 (deck wheel)

    Objective¶

    Break the rigidity of the floor map: 5 rooms, a single direction and no path decisions. The idea is a radial map with the centre as the entrance, rings of rooms and several bosses on the edge, where you can choose which way to go. And it has to stay readable: no crossings, no edges slicing across the map, and with zoom to see it whole or up close.

    What was done¶

    v2 — the star (cf408e4)¶

    • Start centre, 7 rings (5 room + 1 boss) and rooms growing from 5 to 15 per ring.
    • Free, bidirectional movement: you can enter any neighbouring room without visiting it, inwards or outwards. Visited rooms close. If no unvisited neighbour is left, the end-of-run screen appears (END RUN).
    • Each room on a ring links to the 1-2 rooms closest in distance on the outer ring; those left without a door are picked up in a second pass.
    • 3-6 bosses on the outer ring, one per sector.

    The result is much bigger than the floor map (50 rooms), but it does not read: on an ellipse, linking by distance makes many edges tilt and cross (1.4 per map, up to 9), and the boss edges can cross ring 4 again. It looks like a spider web.

    v3 — the rings (231e2cc)¶

    • Linking by angular wedges: each room on the big ring hangs off the room (or boss) on the small ring that is closest in angle. The map ends up split into wedges centred on the small ring, and two roads from different wedges cannot cross.
    • Rooms on the same ring are joined with arcs that follow the ring, never with a straight line that cuts across it.
    • The ring just before the bosses is a complete ring: from any branch you can go around the map and pick another boss.
    • 4-7 bosses, each on the angular centre of its rooms, so that the edges of the last hop do not leave their ring.
    • LateralChance = 0.5 on rings 1-4; ring 5, just before the bosses, is always complete.
    • Zoom and pan: wheel or +/− buttons (60%-220%), drag with the right or middle button and RESET VIEW. The map is clipped inside its area so it does not cover the HUD.

    Room distribution (4bd2079)¶

    • 50% combat, 15% rest, 10% shop and 25% event (previously 62/18/10/10). Ring 1 is still always combat.
    • With 40 rolled rooms (rings 2-5) you get about 10 event rooms and 4 shop rooms per map; combats drop from 29.8 to 25.1 per map.
    • Shop and event are still placeholder screens: for now 35% of the rolled rooms have no real content. The distribution is designed for when they do.

    Bug: the deck wheel moved the map (7f00972)¶

    MapScreen reads the wheel (and the right and middle buttons) directly from Mouse.current, not from the EventSystem. With the deck open, the card scroll also reached the map and zoomed it (and, with the right button, panned it) behind.

    • DeckScreen counts open overlays with a static OpenCount (OnEnable / OnDisable) and MapScreen.Update returns early while there is any.
    • When the deck closes, the counter goes back to 0 and the map recovers wheel and drag.

    Simulation of the three maps¶

    Same benchmark for the three algorithms: 300 seeds × 40 runs = 12,000 traversals per map. Two runs per seed: one random (picks any unvisited neighbour) and one that always looks outwards (if it can, it climbs a ring). The first measures the map as exploration; the second, as a path to the boss. On the floor map there is no difference because there is only one direction.

    What the numbers say

    • v1 is a corridor, not a map. 15 rooms, 2.3 branching points and always 5 rooms of route. The 100% of runs reaching the boss and the 0% of dead ends are not a design merit: they are the consequence of every edge going upwards and every room having one.
    • v2 triples the map but breaks it. Going from 15 to 50 rooms and from 5 to 7.71 rooms of route is exactly what we were after, but 1.44 crossings per map (up to 9) and 2.22 edges passing under another button mean it cannot be read at a glance. In 222 of 300 maps there is at least one such edge.
    • v3 fixes the drawing and gains exploration. 0 crossings and 11.35 rooms per random run (previously 7.71). The price is that one in four random runs ends in a dead end (24.9%) and that there are fewer rooms with two exits (9.0 against 20.0): every room on the big ring has a single radial door and the network closes with arcs.
    • The dead ends are not the bosses' fault. Always going outwards reaches the boss in 6 rooms 100% of the time. Dead ends appear when the player goes inwards or sideways: lateral connections are the lever (LateralChance).
    • The v3 bosses: 4, 5, 6 or 7 bosses in 72, 72, 82 and 74 of the 300 seeds.
    • One remaining blemish: in v3, in 74 of 300 maps an edge of the last hop passes under a neighbouring room's button. It is because the boss sits at the centre of its wedge and an edge room receives a very steep edge. Since the buttons are drawn on top, only a piece of line disappears; it is purely aesthetic.

    Verification¶

    • Simulation: 300 seeds × 40 runs for the three maps. On v3, 0 crossings (worst map: 0), 0 unreachable rooms, 0 overlaps and 0 rooms outside the area; every benchmark assertion passes.
    • Play mode over MCP: map at 100% and at 176% with zoom and pan; 5 of 5 clicks hit the right room at 176%; the zoom buttons go up and down with a 1.12 factor and RESET VIEW returns to 100%; panning stays limited to the area.
    • A run driven always outwards reaches the boss and shows THE HOLLOW OPENS.
    • Wheel bug: in Play mode, with the deck open (OpenCount = 1), injecting 120 wheel units left the zoom at 106% and the map content at (5.77, -102.96); with the deck closed (OpenCount = 0), the same injection raised the zoom to 118% (1.06 × 1.12). No errors or warnings in the console.
    • The Unity console shows no errors after the commits.
    • Captures in %TEMP%\opencode\unity-shots\ (not committed).

    Decisions¶

    • Free, bidirectional movement instead of only outwards: the map is meant to be explored, and going back should not be a punishment. Visited rooms close so that each room has content only once.
    • Dead end = explicit end of run (END RUN), not a silent block. More honest and easier to measure.
    • Angular wedges instead of distance: distance on an ellipse lies (a room can be "closer" to the opposite boss) and generates crossings. Angle is the coordinate that orders the ring and the one that makes the map planar.
    • Arcs for same-ring edges: a straight line between two rooms of the ring cuts the ring and crosses the radial edges; the arc does not.
    • Complete ring before the bosses: it gives one last boss decision without adding any crossing edge.
    • Bosses on the centre of their wedge: it is what stops the edges of the last hop from leaving the ring. The trade-off is that an edge room receives a steep edge that passes under the neighbouring button (0.47 per map).
    • Ellipse 1.45 instead of a circle: makes the most of 16:9 and gives more horizontal separation between rooms.
    • LateralChance as the lever: it is the number that decides how much the player explores and how many dead ends there are; now 0.5.
    • 4-7 bosses (previously 3-6): the outer ring is the last decision; with 4-7 and a complete ring, there is always more than one within reach.
    • A static counter in the deck instead of routing the wheel through the EventSystem: sending zoom to IScrollHandler would be more "correct" in UGUI's eyes, but it would change behaviour across the whole map (the HUD would block scrolling too) and right-button panning is not a UI drag. The counter solves the real case in three lines.
