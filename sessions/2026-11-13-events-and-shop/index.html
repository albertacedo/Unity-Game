    2026-11-13 · Event rooms and shop rooms: gold gets a cost and a sink¶

    Duration: night Commits: 66656ac — event rooms, from the CSV to the screen · 0d321f0 — shop rooms, the second gold sink · this commit — close the session

    Objective¶

    Resolve the two room types the map already rolled but still sent to the placeholder: the events (roadmap #9) and the shop (roadmap #6). The point was to make gold mean something: before this session it was displayed next to the HP and could not be spent.

    What was done¶

    Event rooms¶

    • Rules in Run/EventResolver.cs: one event per event room, picked by weight from events.csv with a roll seeded by the run and the room, and never repeating a seen event while unseen ones remain (RunState.SeenEvents). An option is described with its own numbers, lose_gold is a real cost (greyed out and unclickable when you cannot pay it), healing clamps at max HP, gold clamps at 0, max_hp moves the ceiling and the current HP together, and an option that takes the last HP ends the run.
    • UI/EventScreen.cs only renders: title, body, the options with their price or effect, and a result view with CONTINUE back to the map. Event damage to 0 HP goes to the same end screen as a lost fight.
    • Content: six events in events.csv — shrine, scout, chest, pool, forge, gamble — covering heal, gold, max HP, damage and a gamble.
    • F1: OPEN RANDOM EVENT (the panel grew from 650 to 700 px).

    Shop rooms¶

    • Rules in Run/ShopState.cs: four duplicate-free cards from the reward pool, with a roll seeded by run and room, one bandage per visit, and no gold can go negative.
    • Prices are 30 + 15 × cost, so 0-cost cards cost 30, 1-cost 45 and 2-cost 60. The bandage is 15 HP for 20 gold.
    • UI/ShopScreen.cs renders the four columns (card + price), the bandage and LEAVE, with HP and gold in the header. A bought card reads SOLD; a card you cannot pay for is disabled with its price in red; the bandage explains itself (sold, need 20 gold, you are at full HP).
    • One stock per visit: GameManager keeps the ShopState while the room is open and redraws the screen after each purchase, so buying cannot re-roll the counter. Leaving the room or starting a new run clears it.
    • F1: OPEN SHOP, which opens a fresh stock without needing a room on the map.

    Verification¶

    • tools/compile-check.ps1 → 0 errors, 0 warnings after both features.
    • tools/content-check → 6 events, Content OK.
    • Events, Play mode: -15 gold / +5 max HP landed exactly (99→84 and 72→77); the heal clamped at 72/72; an unaffordable option was disabled and showed its price; a lethal option left HP 0 and the run over at the end screen. Screenshots reviewed.
    • Shop, Play mode: buying the 45-gold card took gold 99→54, the deck 9→10 and the card read SOLD, while the 60-gold card became disabled with 54 gold; with 0 gold all four cards and the bandage were disabled with their reasons; the bandage took HP 40→55 and gold 99→79 and read sold.
    • Real map integration: a generated room (ring 2, index 5, Shop) opened the shop through OnNodeSelected, and LEAVE returned to the map with current = 2/5 Shop visited=True.
    • Unity console after both tests: 0 errors, 0 warnings. shop.png reviewed.

    Decisions¶

    • Two classes per room, not one. The rules (EventResolver, ShopState) are plain C# and the screen only renders them: that is what makes the numbers testable without the Editor, and it is the same split the combat already uses.
    • The stock belongs to the visit. Keeping ShopState in GameManager (instead of rebuilding it on every click) means a purchase cannot re-roll the counter, and the "sold" state survives the redraw.
    • Prices come from the cost, not from a new CSV column. With seven reward cards a price column would be seven copies of the same rule; add it when cards disagree with it. The skill says where the line is.
    • Gold income stays event-only. The shop is a trade-off, not a shopping spree: with 99 gold a run buys one 2-cost card or two 1-cost ones. If prices are ever raised, income has to be raised first.
    • An unaffordable event option is disabled, not auto-clamped. Paying gold is a decision; silently paying what the player has left would hide the cost.
