    Roadmap¶

    Current gaps¶

    1. Gold is useless — it is displayed and cannot be spent (shop pending).
    2. There is only one effect (Vulnerable) and 10 cards: combat still does not have enough decisions per card. The CSV format is ready to grow without touching code.
    3. The map has to be redesigned. The current radial one (fog + LateralChance) worked, but the shape of the run will be decided with the new map: reveal radius, being able to go back and how many rooms a run should last. The 24.9% dead-end and 11.35 rooms-per-run figures no longer rule.

    Priorities¶

    Ordered by value / effort ratio:

    # What Why Effort
    1 Development panel (F1) Makes everything else testable in seconds Low ✅
    2 Deck view + card detail Without it the decisions cannot be evaluated Low ✅
    3 Combat feedback Floating numbers and flashes: changes how it feels Low-mid ✅
    4 Multi-enemy + target selection The biggest jump in depth Mid-high ✅
    5 8 new effects + 15-20 cards Weak, Strength, Dexterity, multi-hit, area… Mid
    6 Shop Gives gold a purpose Mid

    9 Events Narrative variety Mid
    10 Save/continue + manual seed Longer sessions and repeatability Mid
    11 AI art and sound Polish (lots of generation time) High

    The three batches¶

    Batch 1 — make the prototype observable ✅¶

    Dev panel + deck view + combat feedback. It adds no content, but it makes what is already there visible and testable.

    Batch 2 — make combat interesting¶

    Multi-enemy with target selection ✅ + the new effects pack + 15 cards. This is where the game starts to have real decisions.

    Batch 3 — make the run interesting¶

    Shop + relics + rest with a choice. Gold and the map stop being decorative.

    Batch 2b — information fog on the radial map¶

    The map is radial and the player can walk in both directions. The topology is visible, but room type only reveals itself at distance 1; bosses are always visible. Visited rooms do not repeat content and are used to get back to a branch.

    Status (2026-10-28): the playtest works and the fog is accepted. The decisions that were left (provisions, frontier vs. current position, run size) move to the map redesign; they are not closed here.

    • Provisions: if the player explores every branch, make each move consume a provision. Open decision in the new map.
    • Frontier versus current position: for now you can only enter a direct neighbour; nodes connected to other visited rooms are known but cannot be teleported to. It is the point to revisit with "being able to go back".
    • Run size: the LateralChance = 0.55 figures (11.35 rooms per run) stop being the reference; they will be measured on the new map.

    Playtest questions¶

    To answer with the game in hand:

    Combat and rewards (Batch 2 already tested for clicks, targeting and feedback):

    • With 3 energy and 5 cards, do you end combats with energy to spare or unable to do anything?
    • Does a combat last 2-3 turns or 4-6? A normal combat should last 4-6.
    • With 72 HP, do you reach the boss alive or is the map too harsh?
    • Do you always pick the same reward card? If so, the pool is too small.
    • Are the packs (JawWormPack, CultistPack) the right size and damage?

    Map (these will be asked again on the new map):

    • Do you end up over-exploring (too many combats) or do you go straight for the boss? How many rooms per run?
    • Do you miss a limit on exploration, or is health enough already?
    • Is the reveal radius generous enough to decide the route, or do you lack information?
    • Does being able to go back make you explore more or give you agency?
    • Zoom (wheel vs. buttons) and how the outer ring reads: these will be sharpened with the new map.
